# Part-cooling ducts: parametric design and CFD comparison (DD-01)

Owner: Duct-Design & CFD agent. Status: **in progress**. Sections marked *(pending)* are filled in as
the runs finish. Every number in this report tagged [RAN] comes from an OpenFOAM run in this session;
its `results.json` is in `simulations/cfd/results/<case>/`. Anything else is labelled ESTIMATED or
geometric.

> Airflow is not cooling. The isothermal runs show where air goes and how fast it moves near the
> part. The thermal proxy (§9) adds a forced-convection heat-transfer coefficient on a part held at a
> fixed temperature. Neither model predicts print quality; physical tests decide (§12).

## 2. Method recap and deviations from research/04

Solver and settings are those validated in research/04:
- steady incompressible RANS, k-ω SST, `simpleFoam` SIMPLEC;
- the fan as a `fanPressure` P–Q inlet at its outlet face;
- Spalding wall functions;
- L2 production mesh (base cell 2.54 mm, `n_between` 2, surface level 3 on the 1.2 mm duct walls, level 4
  on the nozzle).

Deviations and additions:

| Item | research/04 | This study | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stopping rule | residuals ≤ 1e-3 plus monitors | stop when fan Q and inlet p vary < 0.4 % over 100 iterations (≥ 550 iterations, cap 750); residual caps relaxed to p 1e-2, U 3e-2 | The toolhead flow is weakly unsteady. Free jets around the nozzle and sock leave U residuals on a plateau near 5e-3, and jet-core point probes swing 2–60 % from iteration to iteration. The integrals (Q, ΔP) settle to ±0.2 % [RAN, first S1 run]. |
| Field averaging | snapshot at the last iteration | U, p and wall shear **averaged over iterations 300 → stop** (`fieldAverage`); all metrics use the means | A snapshot of an oscillating jet is not reproducible. Means over ≥ 250 iterations are. |
| Metrics | annulus r 0–5 at Z −0.5, no part | annulus r 1–6 at Z −0.1 above a 20 mm part proxy on the bed; wall regions on the part, nozzle and sock | Lead's specification |
| Thermal | proposed | passive-scalar forced-convection proxy on the converged flow | research/04 §13 |
| Fan curves | ESTIMATED | 4020 at two bounds (ESTIMATED upper; proxy-scaled lower = Same Sky CBM-4020B affinity-scaled); Sunon 5015 proxy-scaled; Delta BFB0524HH DIGITIZED (research/03 §10) | Lead's instruction: 4020 conclusions must hold at both bounds, and decisions are made at the conservative (lower) bound |

Remaining simplifications:
- no carriage, PCB, 3010 fan, cover, extruder or LiDAR module bodies, except in STOCK, which includes the
  stock cover (clipped at Z 32) because its floor is half of the stock duct;

## 5. Design concept, constraints and the candidate set

### 5.1 What the constraints leave

- **Hot-zone clearance.** Nothing within 3 mm of the sock, and nothing below Z 2. Together these exclude
  the whole sock footprint, so every outlet sits outside the rectangle X −14.7…14.8, Y −11.6…11.3. The
  gap between the part top (Z −0.2) and the sock bottom (Z 4.9) is only 5.1 mm. A jet must therefore pass
  under the sock edge at Z < 4.9, starting from an outlet whose lower lip is at Z ≥ 2.2. This limits
  outlets to about Z 3.4…6.4 and inclinations to about 20–35°, and the jets partly graze the sock's
  bottom edge.
- **LiDAR.** `LIDAR_RESERVED_OPTICAL` (X ≤ −18 for Y −36…10, Z −1…22) keeps the left side clear below
  the module. No outlet can sit at the left (180°). The left/rear-left quadrant is reached only by jets
  crossing under the nozzle.
- **Probe.** The MicroProbe body (X ±11, Y 12.5…28.1) blocks the rear (90°).
- **X travel.** The hard limit is X −31…24.2 inside Z 44.3…91.8. This **invalidated the right-bay fan**
  (X 21.5…41.5, Z 22…62) of the first-round layouts, which are listed below. Every fan now sits in the
  front bay, in the stock pose.
- **Front.** `FRONT_LIMIT_Y` −55 (soft). The fan pose shared with the Structure frame (rear face 3 mm in
  front of the frame plate, as in its stub) puts the fan's front face at −56.5. Moving the fan 1.5 mm back
  would shrink the 3010 intake plenum to 1.5 mm.

### 5.2 Duct architecture (all new candidates)

1. **Fan in the front bay.** 4020 at the stock XZ pose, rear face Y −36.5, outlet blowing down at the
   front-right.
2. **Feed drop.** A short vertical transition (contraction 221 → about 150 mm²) into a **front plenum**.
3. **Plenum.** A gable-roofed channel running back (+Y) under the fan into the manifold.
4. **Manifold.** A horseshoe around the sock (azimuth 231.5° → 52°, open on the LiDAR side), 9 mm wide,
   with a 45° gable roof. Its inner-wall height follows what each port needs.
5. **Wedge ports.** Each port has:
   - a vertical outlet face just outside the clearance box;
   - a ceiling rising outward at ≥ 45° and a floor at a shallow angle α_f, so the jet leaves between the
     two and is aimed down and inward;
   - a square-ended top lip, so there is no knife edge.

Printability: every internal ceiling is ≥ 45°, so the duct prints upright (fan socket on top) **with no
support inside the air path**. Face-by-face check: internal overhang area 0 mm². External supports are
needed only under the outer bottom. Wall 1.2 mm; ray-sampled walls below 0.8 mm are only at sharp edges
(0.23 % of samples, against the 0.5 % validator limit). The minimum channel is 3 mm (port height).
Material: PC or PA-CF for the whole nozzle end (DD-07).
